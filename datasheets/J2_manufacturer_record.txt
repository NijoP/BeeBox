6.  FPC routing
❶   FPC should be routed in a manner that no strain or load is exerted onto the FPC. Placing any strain on the 
FPC may result in unintentional disconnect or damage to the FPC, which can lead to issues such as contact 
failure. 
［Caution］
・ Do not allow the FPC or stiffener to touch the casings, housings or any other items.
・ When routing the FPC, make sure that no strain or load is applied to the connector in a pulling, pushing or 
side-to-side motion. Additionally, make sure that no excessive upward or downward force is applied to the
